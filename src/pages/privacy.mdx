# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 5, 2026_

SNLP apps do not collect, transmit, or store your personal data. This page describes what that means in practice and applies to all apps published by SNLP, including **Tada CoverArt / 淘到封面** and **the taikon company / 太空有限公司**.

## Data we collect

None. SNLP apps do not collect any personal data, do not phone home, and do not run analytics.

## Apple Music access

Tada CoverArt reads your Apple Music library to display the albums you've added. The data stays on your device. Nothing about your library is sent to SNLP, to any third party, or to any server.

## Photo Library access

Tada CoverArt writes to your Photo Library only when you tap "Save Album Cover" or "Save Album Art Video". The app does not read your existing photos.

## the taikon company

The game runs entirely on your device. It has no account and no online features, and it sends nothing to SNLP or to anyone else.

Your saves, settings and screenshots are stored on your device, inside the app's own storage. Deleting the app deletes them; you can also delete a single save from the Load screen.

On iPhone and iPad, the game's data (about 3 GB) is downloaded from Apple when you install the app. Apple hosts and delivers that download; SNLP receives nothing from it.

## Third parties

None. SNLP apps do not include any third-party SDKs that collect data.

## Analytics

None. We do not measure crashes, taps, sessions, or any usage metric.

## Children's privacy

SNLP apps are not directed at children under 13.
