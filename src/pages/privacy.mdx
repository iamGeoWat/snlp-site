# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: May 4, 2026_

SNLP apps do not collect, transmit, or store your personal data. This page describes what that means in practice and applies to all apps published by SNLP, including **Tada CoverArt / 淘到封面**.

## Data we collect

None. SNLP apps do not collect any personal data, do not phone home, and do not run analytics.

## Apple Music access

Tada CoverArt reads your Apple Music library to display the albums you've added. The data stays on your device. Nothing about your library is sent to SNLP, to any third party, or to any server.

## Photo Library access

Tada CoverArt writes to your Photo Library only when you tap "Save Album Cover" or "Save Album Art Video". The app does not read your existing photos.

## Third parties

None. SNLP apps do not include any third-party SDKs that collect data.

## Analytics

None. We do not measure crashes, taps, sessions, or any usage metric.

## Children's privacy

SNLP apps are not directed at children under 13.

## Contact

Privacy questions: [solarandlunarpowered@gmail.com](mailto:solarandlunarpowered@gmail.com)

## Changes

If we ever change this policy, we will update the "Last updated" date above and announce the change in the next App Store release notes for any affected app.
